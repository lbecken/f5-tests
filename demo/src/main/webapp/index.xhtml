Becken DateTimePicker

A hybrid JSF component: real UIInput on the server, vanilla JS/CSS widget in the browser, wired together with standard Faces AJAX. Resize the window or open on a phone — the popover becomes a bottom sheet.

Date only & time only

The same component in mode="date" (one click to pick) and mode="time" (two clicks: hour, minute — no clock dial, no wheels).

Constrained range + classic form post
